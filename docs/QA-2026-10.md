# QA round, 2026-10-06

Round 2 (per-result comparison against plugin 1.3.0 and stock, two adversarial reviews, 19 fixes) is in
[REPORT-1.4.0.md](REPORT-1.4.0.md).

Prompted by: "why is it worse than my original compaction benchmarks?" The user meant the upstream engine
benchmarks (`run_battery.py`, `jev_comparison.py`, `threshold_sweep.py`).

## Findings

| # | Finding | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The standalone engine did not regress.** The Tameru arm of the head-to-head corpus gives identical results on 1.3.0 and 1.4.0 with the benchmark's own call shape: mean savings 82.1% on both, all gold kept, 0 distractors. | Verified |
| 2 | **The plugin did regress against that corpus, by a plugin defect.** The task query included the tool's own command (`cat report.txt`). The engine treats a distinctive term (file name, path, identifier) that is absent from the document as "the answer is not here" and fails open. 11 of 14 cases came back uncompressed, fell to the query-blind brief, which kept distractors in 5 cases (including the injected `EXCLUDED-HOST` text) and lost the gold answer in 2. | **Fixed**: `query.usable_query` drops parts naming things absent from the output |
| 3 | The plugin's gates discarded most of what was left: `max_risk` `medium` (the engine labels almost any cut past ~80% "high") and `min_savings` `0.30` (the benchmark's default is 0.10). | **Fixed**: defaults now `high` and `0.10` |
| 4 | **My earlier diagnosis was wrong.** The Phase 5 tuning concluded that the engine's risk label was too coarse and that extracts were rare because of it. The root cause was finding 2. | Corrected here |
| 5 | **My QA could not see it.** The session replay scores cost, error templates and gold facts on its own scenario; it does not score savings or distractors on documents like the benchmark's, and its numbers did not move at all when the defect was fixed. The "battery is byte-identical" check also cannot see log-error loss or filler-query behaviour. | **Added** `tests/test_benchmark_parity.py` (corpus vendored in `tests/fixtures/`): every gold string kept, no distractor, at most 2 results on the brief. Mutation-checked: 7 failures without the fix. |
| 6 | **1.4.0 changed engine behaviour outside the benchmark's view** (intended): filler queries ("continue") now fail open instead of cutting 98%; logs keep one exemplar per error template (a 200 KB log: 99% saved with all errors dropped, now 43% saved with all errors kept); the new test-runner adapter keeps a pytest failure block whole. The old higher savings on those inputs came from dropping error lines. | Documented, not changed |

## Measured: the benchmark corpus through the plugin (13 cases, 12 judged)

Shipped defaults after the fix, each document as a Hermes terminal result asked about by its own question:

- Gold kept and distractors dropped in every judged case; 11 of 12 reach the extract rung.
- Mean savings 67.8% (engine alone on the same cases: about 83%). Before the fix: several cases leaked, two lost the answer.
- Results under 800 characters (`zh_needle`, 561) go to Hermes' own one-line summary, as in stock Hermes.

## Still open

- **Savings gap to the engine alone** (about 15 points): Hermes' elision marker is ~100 characters per gap against `[…]`
  (3), and each row carries a header. Worst: `travis_yaml` (13.5% against 64%), `git_log` (69% against 94%).
- **Short results** (under `min_tool_chars`) keep only Hermes' one-liner, so a short result can lose its answer exactly as
  it does in stock Hermes.
- **Not run in a live Hermes session**; all evidence is the replay, the benchmark corpus and tests.
- Savings are counted in characters here; the engine's own figures use its word-based token estimate.
- Upstream engine: its risk label is too coarse to gate on ("high" covers nearly every deep cut). A recommended change
  belongs in `tameru-compaction-system`, which cannot be pushed from this environment.
